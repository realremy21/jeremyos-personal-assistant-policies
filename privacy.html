Privacy policy

Prelaunch: This service is not open for SMS enrollment. Limits of 5 outbound SMS segments per day and 100 per month, consent, STOP/HELP responses and deletion-request handling are approved proposals and have not been confirmed active. Publishing these pages does not enroll anyone. Retention wording describes the proposed policy; a complete storage and provider-retention review has not been completed.

JeremyOS Personal Assistant processes the enrolled mobile number, consent record, personal-assistant requests, messages and delivery/opt-out records to provide personal reminders and administrative alerts. Twilio and telecommunications carriers process messages for delivery. Mobile numbers are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent are not sold or shared for marketing.

Operator records have no automatic time-based expiry: assistant requests, message/delivery records, authority receipts, consent evidence and suppression history are retained in the private project store while the project operates, until an owner-authorized deletion. STOP ends further messaging but does not delete existing records or suppression history. This project does not promise a 30-day or 90-day automatic purge. Twilio and carriers may retain their own copies under their policies; operator deletion does not guarantee deletion of those copies. Contact 1999jeremyrunnels@gmail.com to request access or deletion; the operator reviews requests before changing records needed for suppression or audit safety.
